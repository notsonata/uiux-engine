Use the UIUX Engine design workflow. Produce the design gate before implementation:

- worst plausible mistake;
- information hierarchy;
- low-fidelity structural wireframe;
- loading, empty, partial, error, success, and offline states;
- interaction and affordance rules;
- form behavior when relevant;
- DESIGN.md constraints;
- type, color, space, and finish decisions;
- responsive and accessibility requirements;
- acceptance criteria.

If implementation is part of the request, continue only after the design gate is explicit.
