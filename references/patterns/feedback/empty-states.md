# Empty States

## Use when
- A collection or surface legitimately has no content.
- Users need to understand why nothing is shown and what to do next.

## Avoid when
- The surface is still loading or failed.
- The absence is self-explanatory and no action is useful.

## Core anatomy
- Reason for emptiness.
- Relevant next action.
- Optional explanation/example.

## State and behavior
- Distinguish first-use, no-results-after-filter, permission-limited, and unavailable-data emptiness.

## Accessibility
- Keep the message and action in normal reading order.
- Illustration must not carry essential meaning alone.

## Responsive behavior
- Keep the action prominent without oversized decorative treatment.

## Decision rules
- Do not use the same copy for first-use and zero-search-results states.
- Avoid filler illustrations when they do not help comprehension.
- Offer a next action only when it is genuinely relevant.
